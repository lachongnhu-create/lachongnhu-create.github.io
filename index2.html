How I work

From question to decision

Whether it's a dashboard, a model or a business process, I follow the same steps.

  1. Scope & Define

    Clarify business objectives, translate stakeholders' requirements into analytical questions, and establish success metrics.

  2. Prepare & Validate

    Clean, structure, and validate raw datasets to ensure data integrity and auditability.

  3. Explore & Analyse

    Conduct exploratory analysis (EDA) using statistical methods and visual tools to discover trends, anomalies, and relationships.

  4. Model & Predict

    Apply machine learning algorithms (e.g., Random Forest, XGBoost, Regression) where predictive modelling drives strategic value.

  5. Communicate & Execute

    Design intuitive Power BI/Tableau dashboards and author executive summaries that bridge technical findings with actionable business strategies.
